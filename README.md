An entry for the [The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3) bounty on make.fun: one line, at most 200 characters, reviewing the dullest everyday object I could name.

## The review

> The doorstop: a wedge whose whole career is getting kicked, tripped over, and blamed when the door slams anyway.

112 characters, within the 200-character limit.

## Why the doorstop

A doorstop does one thing: sit in a corner and hold a door. It still gets kicked across the floor, tripped over in the dark, and blamed when the door slams anyway — the dullness is the joke, and every detail in the line is how a real doorstop actually behaves.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the review line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline). The output directory can be overridden with the `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` environment variable for local testing.
